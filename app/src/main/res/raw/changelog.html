Change Log

1.2.9

  • Call log navigation fixed — D-pad/keypad focus lands on the row itself and no longer gets stuck on the search bar, hamburger menu, or filter chips.
  • Fast scroller — drag the new date handle on the right edge of the call log to jump around quickly.
  • Voicemail fixed — dialing 1 for voicemail now actually places the call.
  • Answer button side — choose whether Answer appears on the left or right for incoming calls (Settings).
  • Change log — see the full version history from the About screen (you're looking at it).
  • Updater now shows the download size before you install.
  • Swipe a call log entry left or right — more actions coming soon.
  • Redesigned the callback icon.
  • Fixed a visual gap in the in-call "More" options panel.

1.2.8

  • Unlimited call history — your call history is now kept forever and no longer gets cut off.

1.2.7

  • Sleep timer, fixed — now reliably hangs up when it should, plus a custom duration option.
  • In-call More menu redesigned with a smoother, expanding look, plus a new Message option.
  • Dial-pad suggestions update instantly when you add or edit a contact.
  • Hebrew T9 — Hebrew contact names now work with the dial pad.

1.2.6

  • Call back list reminders.
  • Fixed missed-call notifications sometimes not appearing.
